Selected research

Peer-reviewed work in analytical chemistry, nanopore sensing, SERS biosensing, and machine-learning diagnostics. Filter by topic below.

First author SERS Multivariate

Surface-enhanced Raman spectroscopy (SERS) and multivariate analysis for elucidating mechanisms of action in antibacterial agents

Der Vang, J. Pahren, E. Duderstadt, F. J. Alvarez, J. Caserta, T. Cambron & Pietro Strobbia

ACS Sensors · 2025

This study applies surface-enhanced Raman spectroscopy (SERS) with multivariate analysis to probe how bacteria respond to different antibacterial agents. By resolving the spectral changes each treatment produces, it helps distinguish mechanisms of action in a faster, lower-cost way than mass spectrometry or genome sequencing.

First author ML diagnostics SERS

Machine learning approaches in label-free small extracellular vesicles analysis with SERS for cancer diagnostics

Der Vang, Maria S. Kelly, Manisha Sheokand, Manju Sharma, Leyla Esfandiari, Ruxandra I. Dima & Pietro Strobbia

bioRxiv · 2024

Small extracellular vesicles (sEVs, or exosomes) carry molecular signatures of their parent cells, making them promising non-invasive biomarkers for cancer. This work pairs label-free surface-enhanced Raman spectroscopy (SERS) with machine-learning classifiers to distinguish cancerous from healthy sEV populations directly from their spectral fingerprints, supporting a rapid, low-cost route toward liquid-biopsy cancer diagnostics.

SERS AI

AI in SERS biosensing: from discriminative to generative

Steven M. Quarin, Der Vang, Ruxandra I. Dima, George Stan & Pietro Strobbia

npj Biosensing · 2025

Artificial intelligence has become central to interpreting the complex spectra produced by SERS biosensors. This perspective traces the field's progression from discriminative models, which classify or quantify analytes, toward emerging generative approaches that can denoise, augment, and simulate spectra, and it discusses the opportunities and open challenges of building trustworthy, AI-driven biosensing.

Nanopore

Next-generation nanopore sensors based on conductive pulse sensing for enhanced detection of nanoparticles

Samuel Confederat, Seungheon Lee, Der Vang, Dimitrios Soulias, Fabio Marcuccio, Timotheus I. Peace, Martin Andrew Edwards, Pietro Strobbia, Devleena Samanta, Christoph Wälti & Paolo Actis

Small · 2023

Resistive-pulse nanopore sensors detect single particles as they translocate a pore, but signal quality can limit sensitivity. This study introduces a conductive pulse sensing (CPS) approach that enhances the detection of nanoparticles, improving signal quality and enabling more reliable label-free, single-particle characterization for biosensing and diagnostic applications.

SERS Point-of-care

Improved point-of-care detection of P. gingivalis using optimized SERS in lateral flow assays

Lyndsay N. Kissell, Daewoo Han, Der Vang, Alexander W. R. Cikanek, Andrew J. Steckl & Pietro Strobbia

Sensors & Diagnostics · 2024

Porphyromonas gingivalis is a key pathogen in periodontal disease, where early point-of-care detection is valuable. This work integrates optimized SERS reporters into lateral flow assays, the familiar format of rapid tests, to substantially improve sensitivity over conventional colorimetric readouts while keeping a simple, field-deployable workflow.

SERS Biosensors

Lyophilizing SERS biosensors to enable translation into an easy-to-use assay

Lutfun Naher, Steven M. Quarin, Der Vang & Pietro Strobbia

Analytical Methods · 2024

A barrier to deploying SERS biosensors outside the lab is their limited stability during storage and transport. This study shows that lyophilizing (freeze-drying) the biosensors preserves their signal and extends shelf life, a practical step toward translating SERS assays into easy-to-use, ready-to-run diagnostic kits.

SERS Hydrogels

Direct detection of tobacco mosaic virus in infected plants with SERS-sensing hydrogels

Lyndsay N. Kissell, H. Liu, Manisha Sheokand, Der Vang, P. Kachroo & Pietro Strobbia

ACS Sensors · 2023

Early, on-site detection of plant viruses can help protect crops. This work develops SERS-sensing hydrogels that directly detect tobacco mosaic virus in infected plants, pointing toward simple, field-deployable diagnostics for plant health.

First author SERS Substrates

Analysis of nanostar reshaping kinetics for optimal substrate fabrication

Der Vang & Pietro Strobbia

Applied Spectroscopy · 2022

Gold nanostars are excellent SERS substrates, but their sharp, signal-enhancing tips reshape over time. This paper analyzes the kinetics of nanostar reshaping to identify conditions that yield stable, reproducible substrates with optimal SERS performance.
